🀄 Mahjong Zen

A calm puzzle, one pair at a time.

How to play

The goal is to clear the board by matching pairs of identical tiles.

1. Select a free tile

A tile is free when no tile is covering it and at least one of its left or right sides is open. Covered or blocked tiles cannot be selected.

2. Match identical tiles

Select one free tile, then select another free tile with the same symbol. The matching pair is removed from the board.

3. Clear the board

Keep finding matching pairs until all tiles have been removed. You win when the board is empty.

Game tools

  • Hint: highlights a matching pair when one is available.
  • Undo: restores the last pair you removed.
  • Shuffle: rearranges the remaining tiles.
  • New game: starts a fresh puzzle.
Tip: Look for pairs that uncover tiles underneath or open up blocked sides. Planning a few moves ahead can help you avoid getting stuck.
