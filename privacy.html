How Mahjong Zen handles information.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Please note: This policy describes the current game and explains what may change if advertising or analytics services are added later. Update this page whenever the site's services or data practices change.

1. About Mahjong Zen

Mahjong Zen is a browser-based tile-matching puzzle. You can play without creating an account.

2. Information stored in your browser

The game may use your browser's local storage to remember your language preference. This preference is stored on your device. The game does not ask you to provide your name, postal address or payment details to play.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The current game may use browser storage for its language setting. If advertising, analytics or other third-party services are added, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. This policy will be updated to identify the services used.

4. Advertising

Advertising is not currently confirmed as active on this site. If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is added, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure or personalize advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. Please consult the provider's privacy information for details.

5. Third-party services and hosting

This website is published using GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process technical information as part of providing and securing its hosting service. Please review GitHub's privacy statement for information about its practices.

6. Your choices

You can clear your browser's site data to remove the saved language preference. You can also adjust cookie and privacy settings in your browser. If advertising is introduced, additional choices may be available through the relevant provider and any consent controls displayed on the site.
