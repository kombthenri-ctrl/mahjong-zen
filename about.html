About Mahjong Zen

Mahjong Zen is a browser-based tile-matching puzzle. The goal is simple: find pairs of identical available tiles and clear the board.

Our goal

We want to offer visitors a straightforward and enjoyable game that can be played directly in a web browser. Take a short break, focus on the board and enjoy finding each matching pair.

Play your way

Use the game tools when you need help: get a hint, undo your last match, shuffle the remaining tiles or start a new game.

Available in two languages: Mahjong Zen offers English and French text so more visitors can enjoy the game.

Thank you for playing

Thank you for visiting Mahjong Zen. We hope you enjoy your time with the game!
